# CampusPulse AI — Data Sources & Telemetry Ingestion Guide

## 1. Data Modes Architecture
CampusPulse AI implements a transparent dual-mode data ingestion architecture configured via the `DATA_MODE` environment variable (`LIVE` or `DEMO`).

```python
# config/settings.py
DATA_MODE = os.environ.get('DATA_MODE', 'DEMO')  # 'LIVE' or 'DEMO'
```

- **`LIVE` (Real-Time Campus Telemetry)**: The ML models ingest active institutional records generated by students, canteen POS terminals, RFID turnstiles, bus GPS, and energy meters.
- **`DEMO` (Calibrated Benchmark Mode)**: Used when live IoT hardware is offline or during academic evaluations. Pipelines ingest standardized benchmark datasets with real mathematical distributions. The UI explicitly presents the badge **`SIMULATED / BENCHMARK DATA`** ensuring academic defensibility.

## 2. Ingested Datasets & Provenance

### 2.1 Academic Performance & Risk
- **Source**: CIT Academic ERP Records & Higher Education Benchmarks (UCI Student Performance Benchmark, Cortez et al.).
- **Attributes**: `attendance_percentage` (0-100), `cgpa` (0.0-10.0), `internal_marks` (0-100), `backlog_count`, `semester`, `quiz_avg`, `assignment_avg`, `lms_hours_avg`, `trend_slope`.
- **Target**: Multi-class academic risk index (`LOW`, `MEDIUM`, `HIGH`).
- **Validation Constraints**: Checked via `ml/utils/validation.py:validate_academic_features`. Out-of-range records rejected.

### 2.2 Dining Hall Demand & Organic Waste
- **Source**: CIT Central Dining Hall point-of-sale registers and kitchen weigh-scales.
- **Attributes**: `day_of_week` (0-6), `meal_type` (Breakfast, Lunch, Snacks, Dinner), `is_event_day`, `student_base`, `meals_prepared`.
- **Targets**: `predicted_demand` (meal counts) and `leftover_waste_kg` (post-service food surplus).
- **Validation**: Strict non-negative constraints, bounded headcount.

### 2.3 Residential Hostel Mess
- **Source**: CIT Residential Hostel registries across 4 distinct halls:
  - `BH-1`: Senior Boys Hall (Base: 380 residents)
  - `BH-2`: Junior Boys Hall (Base: 340 residents)
  - `GH-1`: Senior Girls Hall (Base: 360 residents)
  - `GH-2`: Junior Girls Hall (Base: 320 residents)
- **Target**: Residential meal demand and preparation quota.

### 2.4 Campus Grievance & Facility Tickets
- **Source**: Student natural language complaint submissions.
- **Attributes**: Raw text string ($\ge 5$ characters).
- **Target**: 14 operational departmental categories + automated priority grading (URGENT, HIGH, MEDIUM, LOW).

### 2.5 Bus Fleet Telemetry & Corridors
- **Source**: Campus shuttle GPS waypoints and passenger counter telemetry.
- **Attributes**: `distance_km`, `stop_sequence`, `is_peak_hour`, `passenger_count`, `occupancy_pct`.
- **Target**: Transit duration `travel_time_mins` and schedule `delay_mins`.

### 2.6 Parking Bay Telemetry
- **Source**: Entrance barrier ultrasonic sensors and RFID logs across CIT parking zones.
- **Attributes**: `total_slots`, `hour`, `day_of_week`, `is_event`, `is_weekend`.
- **Target**: Hourly occupancy count and vacancy percentage.

### 2.7 Energy Smart Meters
- **Source**: Sub-metered electrical feeds across departmental laboratory blocks.
- **Attributes**: `hour`, `is_weekend`, `occupancy`, `kwh_consumed`, `baseline`.
- **Target**: Isolation score and fault typology (`NIGHT_LEAK`, `POWER_SURGE`, `NORMAL`).
